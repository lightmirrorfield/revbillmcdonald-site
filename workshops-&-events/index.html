Gatherings Along the Journey

Retreats, conversations, conferences, and spiritual gatherings with Rev. Bill McDonald.

After decades of traveling, teaching, speaking, and serving communities around the world, Rev. Bill continues to gather with seekers in spaces designed for deeper reflection, connection, healing, Love, and spiritual inquiry.

Some gatherings are intimate. Some happen at larger conferences. Some take us into nature.

The setting may change. The invitation remains the same: come as you are, and go a little deeper.

Next Retreat · November 2026

Mount Shasta Spiritual Retreat

Going Deeper
November 6–10, 2026
Wisdom of the Heart Center
Mount Shasta, California

There are times when the spiritual journey asks us to move beyond inspiration and create space to truly listen.

Rev. Bill’s five-day Mount Shasta gathering is an ashram-style immersion for those who feel called toward deeper meditation, spiritual inquiry, self-discovery, connection with nature, and greater awareness of the Divine.

Participants will share long morning meditations, sacred fire and prayer, spiritual teachings, conversations on Love and consciousness, silence, reflection, nature, and practices for opening the heart.

5 Days · $500 Retreat Fee · 19 Acres of Forest · Mount Shasta
