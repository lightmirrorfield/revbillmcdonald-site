Workshops & Events

After years of travelling widely for speaking engagements and self-healing workshops, Rev. Bill has been narrowing his schedule toward longer retreats — fewer rooms, more time, deeper work.

Mount Shasta, California

Spiritual Family Reunion

Several days at the Wisdom of the Heart Center in the shadow of Mount Shasta, exploring love, forgiveness, gratitude and unity, with particular attention to self-forgiveness as the gate the rest passes through. Rev. Bill has described it as a spiritual slumber party in an ashram-style setting, and also as a boot camp for spiritual warriors. Morning and evening meditation, satsang, energy healing, yoga, hiking, and long unhurried conversation.

He is joined by friends who bring their own gifts to the circle — among them Mount Shasta astrologer and shaman Eric Starwalker, sound and energy healer Kana Koinuma, and Pontea Dianati, PhD, who works in soul psychology and energy healing.
