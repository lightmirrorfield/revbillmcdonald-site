Mount Shasta Spiritual Retreat

Going Deeper
Wisdom of the Heart Center · Mount Shasta, California

Five days away from the noise of everyday life, with space for longer meditation, sacred fire and prayer, spiritual inquiry, conversations on Love and consciousness, silence, nature, and practices for opening the heart.

The retreat is held on 19 acres of old-growth forest at the foot of Mount Shasta and is designed as an intimate, ashram-style experience.
