The Invitation

There are moments when the spiritual path asks something more of us.

Not another inspirational weekend. Not simply another collection of teachings. Not a temporary escape from ordinary life.

Sometimes we are called to step away from the familiar and enter a quieter place where the deeper questions can finally be heard.

This is for those who feel ready to go beneath the surface. To meditate longer. To listen more deeply. To open more fully to love, and become more conscious of the sacred energy moving through our lives.

Your Guide

Rev. Bill McDonald

Spiritual Warrior · Author · Vietnam Veteran

Reverend Bill’s life has been a spiritual journey spanning eight decades, a mystical trip in search of gurus, the paranormal, and self-discovery. He has written about his many spiritually transforming experiences, including three near-death experiences and supernatural events during his combat tour of duty in the Vietnam War.

He is an author, an award-winning poet, an international motivational speaker, an artist, a film advisor, and a veteran advocate — and the author of Warrior: A Spiritual Odyssey and Alchemy of a Warrior’s Heart, among other books.

The Land

The Setting

We gather at Wisdom of the Heart Center, nestled among old-growth forest at the foot of Mount Shasta. Wagon Creek flows through the land, and the mountain watches over the entire experience.

Mount Shasta, seen from the surrounding land

The trees are old. The creek keeps moving. The mountain remains still. Somewhere between those two movements, we may discover something within ourselves that has been quietly waiting to be noticed.
