An Invitation From Rev. Bill

There Are Places That Invite Us to Remember

There are places in this world that seem to reach beyond the ordinary. Places where the air feels different, where the mountains seem to breathe, and where something deep within us remembers a connection we may have forgotten.

Mount Shasta is one of those places.

I invite you to join me for five extraordinary days at the base of this magnificent and mystical volcano as we come together in the spirit of healing, awakening, fellowship, and Divine Oneness.

AWAKENING THE SACRED LIGHT WITHIN is more than a spiritual retreat. It is an opportunity to step away from the noise, demands, and distractions of everyday life and take a journey inward. A journey toward rediscovering the peace, love, and healing that have always existed within us.

Hosted by Rev. Bill McDonald and co-hosted by a wonderful gathering of spiritual teachers, healers, and fellow seekers, we will create a sacred space where the body can rest, the mind can become still, and the soul can breathe again.

The mountain is the setting.The heart is the destination.

Five days to slow down, breathe deeply, listen inwardly, and reconnect with the sacred Light already within you.

The Experience

Five Days to Step Away and Go Within

Stillness

Morning meditation, silence, reflection, and space to listen inwardly.

Sacred Fire

Gathering before sunrise around the warmth and light of the fire.

Healing & Release

Exploring forgiveness, self-love, gratitude, old wounds, and burdens we may be ready to release.

Connection

Nature, spiritual conversation, shared experience, laughter, tears, fellowship, and community.

Together, we will celebrate the LIGHT, the LOVE, and the ONENESS that connect all living things. We will share our wisdom, our experiences, our laughter, our tears, and perhaps a few moments of unexpected magic.

We will learn from one another, support one another, and rediscover the beauty of being together as one spiritual family.

Before the World Awakens

We Gather Around the Fire

Our days will begin in a very special way.

In the early morning darkness, before the world awakens, we will gather around the sacred fire pit inside our retreat house.

There, surrounded by the warmth and magical glow of burning logs, we will sit together in meditation as the first rays of morning sunlight slowly find their way through the windows.

Imagine the silence.

The warmth of the fire.

The stillness of the morning.

The faces of fellow travelers gathered in a circle, sharing a moment that requires no words.

We will welcome the new day with open hearts, quiet minds, and gratitude for the simple miracle of being alive.

The Inner Journey

Healing Beyond the Surface

Throughout our five days together, we will explore the many dimensions of healing. Not simply the healing of physical ailments, but the deeper healing that comes from releasing old wounds, forgiving ourselves and others, letting go of unnecessary burdens, and making room for a greater expression of love.

We will explore the power of self-love, forgiveness, gratitude, and the realization that we are not separate from the Divine Presence we spend so much of our lives searching for.

Perhaps the greatest healing begins when we stop looking outside ourselves for something we believe is missing and begin discovering the sacred presence that has been quietly waiting within us all along.

This is the heart of our gathering: to awaken the sacred light within, to recognize the Divine in ourselves and in one another, and to experience the Oneness that connects all living things.

Come With an Empty Cup

Come with an empty cup. Leave behind the need to know all the answers. Set aside the expectations, labels, and preconceived ideas that so often stand between us and genuine spiritual discovery.

You do not need to belong to a particular religion, follow a particular teacher, or believe in any particular spiritual tradition.

You simply need a willingness to look inward, an open mind, and a heart that is ready to receive.

The Place

Why Mount Shasta?

Mount Shasta has a special way of inviting us into that experience. Its magnificent presence, its natural beauty, and the extraordinary sense of peace that so many visitors experience make it a wonderful setting for this inward journey.

But there is something important I want each of us to remember.

The mountain may inspire us. The sacred fire may bring us together. Our teachers and fellow seekers may share their wisdom, experiences, and love. But the real work happens within each individual.

Beyond the Five Days

The Retreat Does Not End When We Leave the Mountain

The true measure of this retreat will not simply be what we experience during these five days, but what we carry home with us when we leave.

Will we return to our everyday lives with a little more patience,
a little more compassion,
a greater capacity to forgive,
and a deeper appreciation for the people who share this journey with us?

Will we look at ourselves and the world around us a little differently?

Will we remember to live the very things we came here to discover?

That is where the healing begins.

The Heart of the Work

Love. Forgiveness. Gratitude.

Ultimately, this is what my ministry has always been about. Teaching LOVE. Practicing FORGIVENESS. Living in GRATITUDE.

Not as beautiful words to be repeated, but as spiritual principles to be lived every day.

Because the Divine is not something we need to find somewhere outside ourselves. The Divine is already present within us, within one another, and throughout all of creation. Our journey is learning to recognize that presence and allowing it to guide the way we live.
