Privacy Policy

How information is handled on this site
Last updated September 2026

Rev. Bill McDonald and Spiritual Warrior Ministries respect your privacy and are committed to treating your personal information with care.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit this website and how that information may be used.

Information You Choose to Share

You may voluntarily provide information such as your name, email address, or other contact information when you:

  • contact Rev. Bill through the website
  • subscribe to emails or reflections
  • inquire about retreats, speaking engagements, mentoring, or events
  • register for an offering or gathering

We use this information only for the purpose for which it was provided, such as responding to your message, providing requested information, or sending communications you have chosen to receive.

Website Information

Like many websites, this site may collect limited technical information such as browser type, device type, pages visited, approximate location, or website traffic data.

This information may be collected through website hosting services, analytics tools, or similar technologies and is generally used to understand how the website is being used and to improve the visitor experience.

Email Communications

If you choose to join Rev. Bill's email list, you may receive occasional reflections, teachings, event information, retreat announcements, or other updates.

You may unsubscribe at any time using the unsubscribe link provided in the email.

Sharing of Information

Personal information is not sold or rented to advertisers.

Information may be processed by trusted service providers that help operate the website, email communications, event registrations, payment processing, or related services. These providers may receive only the information necessary to perform those functions.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law.

External Websites

This website includes links to books, interviews, organizations, teachers, practitioners, resources, social media platforms, and other third-party websites.

Once you leave this website, the privacy practices of the external website apply. Rev. Bill McDonald and Spiritual Warrior Ministries are not responsible for the privacy policies or practices of third-party websites.

Your Choices

You may contact us to request that personal information you have directly provided be corrected or deleted, subject to applicable legal or administrative requirements.

Cookies & Analytics

This website may use cookies or similar technologies to understand general website traffic, improve site performance, and provide certain website functions.

Some third-party services embedded or linked from the site, such as YouTube, email services, social media platforms, or analytics providers, may also use cookies according to their own privacy policies.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically as the website and its services evolve.
