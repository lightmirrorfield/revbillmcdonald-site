# Article image prompts and how the artwork was produced

**Production method.** The base artwork for all three reflections was drawn with code (generative composition: gradients, light, ribbons, bokeh and film grain) rather than an AI image model. This keeps the palettes exact and guarantees there are no faces, no stray symbols and no invented Sanskrit anywhere in the images. Typography was added afterwards in Cormorant Garamond and IBM Plex Mono, the same fonts the site uses.

The prompts below are the creative briefs the artwork was built to, written so the same imagery can be re-created or extended with an image generator if ever needed. Each asks for **no text, no faces, no symbols**.

## 1. Shakti: Awakening the Divine Feminine Within Us
**Hero / base art (16:9; also made 4:5, 1:1 and 9:16 with a calmer top for type):**
Abstract cinematic editorial artwork. A vast, still field of deep midnight-indigo; from a lotus-like base, flowing silk-like ribbons of warm vermillion and antique-gold light rise and twist outward like consciousness becoming creation. Soft sunrise-amber glow along the lower edge. Balance of stillness and movement; sacred but sophisticated, strong yet nurturing. Matte film grain, painterly depth, calm negative space for typography. No people, no faces, no text or letters, no symbols, no mandalas, no chakra diagrams, no temples, no neon, no purple.
**Alt text:** Abstract editorial artwork representing stillness and flowing creative energy in indigo, vermillion and gold.

## 2. Spiritual Performance vs. Spiritual Transformation
**Hero / base art:**
Quiet, austere cinematic interior. A bare meditation room in the foreground with one plain cushion on a wooden floor, rim-lit by warm ivory light spilling through a doorway; far beyond the doorway, softly blurred, a tiny distant stage with a single spotlight. Midnight blue, charcoal, warm ivory, restrained antique gold. Contemplative, not satirical. No people, no text, no screens, no religious icons, no crystals, no neon. Shallow depth of field, matte grain, negative space for typography.
**Alt text:** Quiet meditation space contrasted with a distant stage-like light, representing inner transformation and outward performance.

## 3. The Chosen One Trap
**Hero / base art:**
Cinematic night field of many small warm flame-like lights receding to the horizon. One flame sits a little nearer and brighter, but the composition opens outward to hundreds of identical lights sharing the same glow at the same level. Midnight blue, warm amber, antique gold, charcoal, a touch of ivory. No people, no crowns, no halos, no text, no symbols. Serene, matte grain, calm sky for typography.
**Alt text:** A field of individual lights sharing the same glow, representing service without spiritual hierarchy.
