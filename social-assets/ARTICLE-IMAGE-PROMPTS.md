# Article imagery

The hero photographs for the three reflections were supplied for this project (provided as 1.webp, 2.webp and 3.webp). Every website and social image was composed from them. The lettering was added afterwards in Cormorant Garamond and IBM Plex Mono, the same fonts the site uses.

| Reflection | Photograph | Alt text used on the site |
|---|---|---|
| Shakti | Red silk, a brass lotus-shaped lamp and floating petals at sunrise | A brass lotus-shaped oil lamp glowing beside red silk and floating petals at sunrise, a symbol of the Divine Feminine. |
| Spiritual Performance vs. Spiritual Transformation | A person seated before an antique mirror in a quiet room | A person seated in a quiet room, looking at her reflection in an antique mirror, with candles and a meditation cushion nearby. |
| The Chosen One Trap | A hand lighting one more candle among many | A hand lighting one more candle among many glowing candles, representing service among equals. |

**Layouts.** Open Graph and Instagram cards crop the photo and set text over a darkened edge. Reel covers keep the whole photo sharp in a band over a softened backdrop. The Spiritual Performance Instagram card also uses the band layout so the text never covers the face. Quote cards are square crops with text over the darker top area.

**No likeness of Rev. Bill** appears in any of the artwork.

## Files
Per article (names as requested): hero (1600x900 JPG + WebP), og (1200x630), instagram (1080x1350), reel or reel-cover (1080x1920), quote card(s) (1080x1080). Hero and og files also live in `/images/` for the website; the complete set is in this folder.
