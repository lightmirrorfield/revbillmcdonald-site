Books From the Journey

Stories, Poetry & Wisdom

Eight books gathered across a lifetime of searching

Cosmic Chronicles

February 27, 2025

A collection of spiritual essays drawing on his life as a Vietnam veteran, healer, poet, and near-death experiencer. He writes on overcoming fear, the power of forgiveness, reincarnation, and the energy of love — bridging the metaphysical and the practical in plain, accessible language.

Purchase from Amazon.com

Spiritual Prayers & Blessings

December 8, 2024

Reverend Bill McDonald offers a heartfelt collection of original prayers and blessings that transcend the boundaries of any single religion. Rooted in a universal spirituality, these prayers are directed to the ONE God—the Creator of all—making them accessible to everyone, regardless of their faith background.

Purchase from Amazon.com

Sacred Verses

December 4, 2024

Poetry and prose as a record of a spiritual journey. He describes these pieces as passageways into his own interior — written from the space between thoughts and words, moving through joy, peace, pain, and suffering toward a single theme of oneness.

Purchase from Amazon.com

I Remember Tomorrow

November 19, 2024

"I Remember Tomorrow" unfolds as a profound exploration of love and forgiveness, guiding readers on a personal spiritual journey. Rev. Bill McDonald shares his transformative experiences over the past decade, navigating the challenges of cancer, heart attacks, and the societal shifts brought by COVID-19.

Purchase from Amazon.com

Alchemy of a Warrior’s Heart

July 31, 2018

An old Vietnam War veteran learns about his life and why he is here. He shares his life changing experiences while traveling four times to India in search of meaning and understanding. He spends months at a time in isolation at a jungle ashram with his newly found Satguru. His memoir covers a decade after he retires from his government work.

Purchase from Amazon.com

Warrior: A Spiritual Odyssey

August 14, 2015

The autobiography. It begins in a difficult childhood, moves through an astonishing tour of duty in Vietnam, and opens out into a life of unusual breadth. Selected stories rather than a straight chronology — and it ends, as his own search did, standing at Machu Picchu.

Purchase from Amazon.com

Sacred Heart: Poetry in Search of the Divine

March 1, 2004

A five decade collection of his spiritual and life insights captured in prose and poetry by Rev. Bill. A national award winning book — Publisher’s Poetry Book of the Year, 2004 ($25,000 marketing prize).

Purchase from Amazon.com

Purple Hearts: Poetry of the Vietnam War

January 6, 2004

A collection of prose and poetry written during and after the Vietnam War. A reflective narrative of emotions and spiritual growth through words by a Purple Heart combat veteran of that war.

Purchase from Amazon.com
