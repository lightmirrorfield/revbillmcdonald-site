Support the Work

Help Carry a Lifetime of Love and Service Forward

For decades, Rev. Bill McDonald has devoted much of his life to sharing a simple message: Love more. Forgive. Serve. Remember the Divine in one another.

Through conversations, healing-oriented work, spiritual gatherings, mentorship, writing, retreats, and countless personal encounters, Rev. Bill has continued to offer what life has taught him.

Much of this work has been given freely, quietly, and without making money the center of the exchange. As the work continues, there are many ways to participate in it.

You may come to learn. You may come to receive. You may come to gather with others. You may help share the message. Or you may simply help make it possible for the work to continue.

Every sincere form of participation helps carry the message forward.

The Exchange

Receiving and Giving

Spiritual service and material life do not exist in separate worlds.

Travel has costs. Retreats and gatherings require resources. Websites, recordings, books, events, and the preservation of decades of teachings require practical support. And every human being, including those who spend much of their lives serving others, has ordinary material needs.

Supporting Rev. Bill’s work helps create the practical foundation from which the work can continue. But the spirit of the exchange matters.

Give because something has touched your heart.

Participate because you feel called.

Never because you feel pressured or obligated.

Why I Felt Called to Create This Page

A Personal Reflection

Over the past few months, I have had the blessing of coming to know Rev. Bill more closely, and I continue to feel deep gratitude for the way this connection entered my life. For me, it has felt like a Divine orchestration.

What touched me was not simply Rev. Bill’s extraordinary life experiences or the depth of his spiritual journey. It was his humility, childlike openness, warmth, and the sense of safety and unconditional Love that I experience in his presence.

As I gradually came to understand more about the way Rev. Bill has served people over many decades, something became increasingly clear to me: those who carry a message of Love also deserve to be supported.

Rev. Bill is far more likely to give than to ask. And perhaps that means that sometimes those of us who have received something meaningful through his presence, teachings, writings, healing-oriented work, mentorship, or gatherings can choose to support the work without waiting to be asked.

Not from obligation. From gratitude. Not because spiritual wisdom has a price. But because spiritual work still takes place in a material world.

My hope in creating this page is simply to make the doorway available. If Rev. Bill’s work has touched you, support it in whatever way genuinely feels right for you.

With Love and gratitude,

Ragini

Passing the Light Forward

Mentoring the Next Generation

A lifetime of experience eventually raises another question: what do we pass forward?

Rev. Bill’s mentorship work is not about reproducing another version of himself. It is about encouraging those called to serve to deepen their own integrity, spiritual foundation, discernment, courage, humility, and relationship with the Divine.

The hope is not to create followers. It is to help create people capable of standing in their own truth and serving others from Love.
