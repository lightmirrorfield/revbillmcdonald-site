A Life Transformed by Love

From War to Love

The journey of a Vietnam veteran, mystic, and lifelong seeker who discovered that the deepest spiritual teaching may also be the simplest.
Veteran · Mystic · Messenger of Love
Discover His Journey Watch His Story

Rev. Bill’s story cannot easily be placed into a single category. He has been a soldier, a decorated Vietnam veteran, a husband, a father, an author, a poet, a minister, a teacher, a spiritual seeker, and a mystic. Yet beneath all of these identities is a remarkably simple message: love, forgive, be grateful, heal, and remember our oneness.

1968

Vietnam

A young helicopter crew chief and door gunner, confronting war, mortality, trauma, and the fragility of life.

Today

A Message of Love

After decades of spiritual searching, mystical experience, healing, gratitude, forgiveness, service, and reflection, Rev. Bill’s message has become remarkably simple: Love. Forgive. Be Grateful. Heal. Remember our Oneness.

Somewhere between those two moments lies an extraordinary journey.

His Philosophy

The Message Is More Important Than the Messenger

After a lifetime of extraordinary experiences, Rev. Bill does not ask people to believe in him or follow him. His invitation is simpler: discover love, forgiveness, healing, and the Divine within your own life.

The teacher can point toward the door. The journey remains your own.
